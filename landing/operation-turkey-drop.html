Mission Briefing

How It Works

Clean home. Full table. It is the same simple plan every time — and every booking multiplies into ten meals on a Nanaimo family's table.

  1. Book Your Service

    Pick a date for your driveway, siding, roof, gutters or windows. Normal pricing — no premium, no upsell.

  2. We Fund 10 Dinners

    Every completed job funds ten fresh turkey dinners, prepared locally by Real Meals Nanaimo.

  3. We Deliver Together

    Our crew loads the van and drops the meals with local families and partner organizations across Nanaimo.

Mission FAQ

Do I have to book a service to help?

No. You can donate any amount directly through GoFundMe, and 100% of it goes toward turkey dinners. Booking a service is simply the option that gets your home cleaned and funds ten dinners at the same time.

What services can I book?

House washing, driveway and concrete cleaning, roof and gutter cleaning, deck and patio washing, and window cleaning throughout Nanaimo and the surrounding area. Pick your service on the booking page.

Who receives the turkey dinners?

Local families and individuals referred through our community partners in Nanaimo. If you know a household that should be on the list, reach out through the booking form and let us know.

Where does the money actually go?

Straight into food and delivery. Meals are prepared locally by Real Meals Nanaimo, and our crew handles the drops in our own vans on our own time.

When are the dinners delivered?

Deliveries run in the days leading up to Thanksgiving. Book or donate early so we can lock in the meal count with our kitchen partner.
